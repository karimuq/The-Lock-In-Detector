# The Lock In Detector
A desktop utility to help you stay focused by detecting when you are looking at your phone.
The Lock In Detector uses AI to ensure you stay focused by automatically detecting if you pull out your phone!

**Features:**
- 100% Offline (No data is sent anywhere)
- Real-time webcam tracking
- "Punishes" you if you get distracted by opening a job application

**How to run:**
Just download `TheLockInDetector.exe` below, open it, and click Start!
